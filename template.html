How to vote

Voting is compulsory

If you are enrolled, you must vote. Polls are open 8am to 6pm on Saturday 28 November.

No photo ID needed

You do not need photo ID to vote in Victoria. You will be asked for your name and address.

Number every box

For your local MP (the Legislative Assembly ballot), number every box in order of preference. Leave one blank and the vote does not count.

Find your polling place

The VEC website shows where to vote and the other ways to vote.

Rules and dates can change, so check the Victorian Electoral Commission for the latest.

Victoria Votes 2026

An independent, unofficial guide to the Victorian state election on Saturday 28 November 2026. Every win chance is a model estimate from the polls, not an official forecast or a poll of that seat. No tracking or accounts: a saved seat stays on your device.

How it works

Each pollster’s latest numbers are turned into seat chances using the 2022 results, then blended by how recent, large and independent each poll is. It refreshes daily.

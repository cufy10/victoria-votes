This is a list of the '''candidates of the [[2026 Victorian state election]]'''.

There are 734 Legislative Assembly candidates, down from a total of 740 in 2022. The 303 candidates for the Legislative Council was the lowest number since the 2010 election.<ref>{{cite web | url=https://antonygreen.com.au/vic2026-candidate-numbers-at-close-of-nominations/| title=VIC2026 – Candidate Numbers at Close of Nominations}}</ref>
